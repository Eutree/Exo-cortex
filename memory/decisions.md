# Decisions

One row per decision, newest at the bottom. The **reason** column is the one that matters: in three
months nobody remembers why X was chosen over Y, and without it the decision gets argued again.
`/close` writes these rows; you can also add one by hand. Dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`: the monthly quiz
reads them.
